    Applications

    From captured data
    to training data.

    Three benchmarks measure how well today’s models understand these scenes. Four demonstrations turn scans and recordings into simulator scenes and robot trajectories.

    Hover a use to see what it takes. For reference, teleoperating a real robot needs the robot, an operator and a reset for every episode.

    Understand · Benchmarks

    Three benchmarks.
    Ten scenes.

    Existing models, evaluated on the same 10 scanned scenes. No single model leads on every metric, and more input views do not consistently help.
