    Applications

    From captured data
    to training data.

    Hover a use to see what it takes. For reference, teleoperating a real robot needs the robot, an operator and a reset for every episode.

    Understand · Benchmarks

    Three benchmarks.
    Twenty scenes.

    Existing models, evaluated on the same 20 scanned scenes. No single model leads on every metric, more input views do not consistently help, and precise 3D boxes and metric distances are still out of reach.

    Input views
    Metric

    Categories in, 3D boxes out.

    Annotators labelled the category and 3D box of every object in the 20 scenes. A method is given the categories that occur in a scene and returns their 3D boxes, metric and in the scene’s gravity-aligned frame.

    Best on all three metrics: Boxer, which lifts open-vocabulary 2D detections to 3D boxes. Every other method stays under 7 AP25, and LLaVA-3D and SceneScript place no box at IoU 0.25.No method reaches 7 AP50: precise 3D boxes in real, cluttered scenes are still beyond current models.

    AP25 and AP50: average precision at a 3D IoU of 0.25 and 0.5. mIoU@0.25: the mean 3D IoU of the predictions matched to a reference box at IoU 0.25 (0 where none match). All in %. *LLaVA-3D: the public checkpoint, its boxes read from its text output.

    500 questions, six kinds.

    Question–answer pairs written on the 20 scenes, scored by answer accuracy for each kind and overall. One example of each kind, written for this page:

    Best overall: Gemini-2.5-Pro, 74.2%. HoliWorld-VL-4B reaches 67.8%, above every open-weight model (at most 60.4%) and GPT-5 High (65.8%).Every model answers at least 74% of the height and relative-position questions; none passes 34% on distance or 55% on translation.

    Answer accuracy in %; Overall is over all 500 questions. 23 are multiple choice and 84 take a number. Object names and equivalent directions are normalised; a distance counts as correct within max(0.1 m, 10% of the true value).
